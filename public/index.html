Security first

Your wallet secrets stay yours.

ChainRestore does not need your seed phrase, private key, wallet password or remote access to investigate public transaction data. Never share these secrets with an agent or support representative.

Blockchain tracing can identify transaction paths, but it does not guarantee that assets can be recovered. Any recovery outcome depends on the facts, intermediaries and applicable legal processes.
