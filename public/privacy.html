Privacy notice

ChainRestore stores account details provided by its sign-in provider and incident records submitted by signed-in users. Case records are scoped to the account that created them. Do not submit wallet secrets, passwords or one-time codes. Public transaction references and incident descriptions may be used to provide educational and documentation support. Contact the site administrator for access or deletion requests.

This initial notice is a launch placeholder and should be reviewed and expanded to reflect the final operator, contact details, retention periods and applicable privacy law before public marketing.
