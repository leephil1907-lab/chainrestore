Privacy Policy

This policy explains what ChainRestore collects, why it is used, how incident records are protected and what choices you have.

Effective: 10 November 2026·Applies to ChainRestore website and member tools

1. Information we handle

When you sign in, ChainRestore processes your email address and sign-in security information. If you submit an incident report, we process the blockchain network, incident category, optional public transaction reference, optional incident date and the description you choose to provide.

Do not submit seed phrases, private keys, wallet passwords, one-time codes, identity documents or unrelated sensitive information.

2. How we use information

Information is used to authenticate your account, save and display your incident records, respond to support requests you initiate, maintain platform security and improve the reliability of the service.

We do not need access to your wallet or authority to move your assets.

3. Database access and isolation

Incident records are associated with the authenticated account that created them. PostgreSQL row-level security is enabled and forced on the case table, and every case query also filters by the authenticated user ID. Anonymous visitors cannot access member case endpoints.

Administrative infrastructure access is limited to authorised platform maintainers for operation and security. Access should be logged and limited to what is necessary.

4. Retention and deletion

Records are retained while needed to provide the account and case-history features, maintain security and meet applicable obligations. You may contact the platform administrator to request access, correction or deletion. Requests may require account verification.

Do not assume that closing a browser session deletes a case record.

5. Children and age limit

ChainRestore is not intended for children under 13. The sign-in flow asks for a date of birth before sending an authentication request. If the entered age is under 13, access is blocked and the date of birth is not sent to the server, stored in the browser or added to an account.

Users must not attempt to bypass this age restriction.

6. Cookies and local storage

Authentication uses an essential HttpOnly, Secure and SameSite session cookie. The age-check date is processed in the page only; after an eligible user confirms, the browser stores only a temporary session flag. The age-check date is processed in the page only and is not saved by ChainRestore. We do not use it to build an advertising profile.

7. Service providers

Render provides application hosting and managed PostgreSQL. Resend delivers sign-in verification emails. These providers process information as needed to operate the website. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to protect users, the service or the public.

8. Security

We use account authentication, access controls and owner-scoped case queries. No online service can promise absolute security. Use a unique email account password, keep verification codes private and report suspected account misuse promptly.

9. No wallet custody or guaranteed recovery

ChainRestore does not ask you to connect a wallet, provide a seed phrase or private key, or transfer assets to us. Blockchain tracing and educational guidance do not guarantee that lost assets can be recovered. Always verify official exchange, wallet-provider and authority contact channels independently.

10. Contact and policy updates

For privacy requests or questions, use the support route on this website. This policy must be updated with the legal operating entity and verified privacy contact details before a full commercial launch. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
