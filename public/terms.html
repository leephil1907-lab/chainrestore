Terms of Use

These terms explain the current scope of ChainRestore and the conditions for using its educational and incident-documentation tools.

Effective date: 10 November 2026

1. Service scope

ChainRestore provides blockchain education, incident documentation and general security guidance. It is not a bank, exchange, custodian, law firm, law-enforcement agency or guaranteed asset-recovery service.

2. No guaranteed recovery

Blockchain tracing may help explain public transaction activity but does not reverse transactions or guarantee that funds can be retrieved. You remain responsible for promptly contacting the relevant exchange, wallet provider, authorities and qualified professionals.

3. Protect wallet credentials

Never submit or disclose seed phrases, private keys, wallet passwords, one-time codes or remote-device access. ChainRestore does not need custody of your wallet or permission to move assets. Stop communicating with anyone who requests these secrets.

4. Accounts and private records

You are responsible for keeping access to your email account secure. Incident records are intended to be visible only to the authenticated account that created them. Do not attempt to access another person's account or records.

5. Age requirement

ChainRestore is not intended for children under 13. The sign-in page checks the date of birth in the browser before any authentication request. If the age entered is under 13, the sign-in process is blocked and that date is not sent to or saved by ChainRestore.

There is currently no paid subscription, recurring membership plan or automatic renewal offered through this website. No subscription price, renewal charge or cancellation fee currently applies.

If subscriptions are introduced, the total price, billing frequency, renewal timing, cancellation route and any applicable fees will be clearly disclosed before payment is authorised. No recurring charge should be created without the user's express confirmation.

7. Acceptable use

Use the website lawfully and provide accurate information to the best of your knowledge. Do not impersonate an exchange, wallet provider, regulator, law-enforcement officer or ChainRestore representative. Do not use the platform to deceive, threaten or exploit others.

8. Availability and changes

Features may change as the service develops. We may restrict access where needed to protect users, data or platform security. Material changes to these terms will be reflected on this page.
