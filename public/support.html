Clear your doubts safely

Educational call requests are available to members. Do not share passwords, one-time codes, private keys or seed phrases during a call. Support calls explain information and next steps; they do not guarantee that funds can be recovered.

Unlimited live calls are not advertised as a confirmed service until staffing and scheduling are configured. No website setup charge is applied.
